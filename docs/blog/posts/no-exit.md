---
title: "No Exit"
date: 2018-07-24
---

Jean-Paul Sartre's iconic play comes to life in this multimedia extravaganza, with television screens broadcasting the characters' thoughts and past lives as they suffer for all eternity with each other.

The three video screens play random clips from prerecorded material, generating a unique and live spontaneous edit each night. Randomizer video controller written in [Processing](https://processing.org/) and activated with [QLab](https://figure53.com/qlab/) via OSC messages. Playback executed with VLC via telnet remote control.

Produced by [Big Idea Theatre](http://www.bigideatheatre.org/) - July 2015.

Scope provided:

- Video design
- Randomizer video player
