Projection design videos for [Rocklin Community Theatre](http://rocklintheatre.org)’s production of 1776! The Musical!

Cues include the debated portions of Jefferson’s draft, animated signatures of the declaration, and the letters from George Washington.

<div class="video"><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/dGFltbfYNo4" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

List of cues:

- Calendar flip (all dates referenced in script, May 30 - July 4)
- G. Washington Letter #1
- G. Washington Letter #2
- G. Washington Letter #3
- “Mama, Hey Mama” - battle sequences
- Jefferson’s draft - beginning
- Jefferson’s draft - end
- Draft - “Scotch and Foreign mercenaries…”
- Draft - “For denying us the benefits…”
- Draft - “Tyrant…”
- Draft - “Unfeeling Brethren…”
- Draft - “Parliament”
- Draft - The Slavery Passage
- Colonial Flag Waving - “Is Anybody There”
- Hancock’s signature
- G. Washington Letter #4
- Signing the Declaration / Finale

All cues in order can be viewed on [this playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLQXhyEKaLkHHWvx9rX-FpNYx65lKe00bM).
