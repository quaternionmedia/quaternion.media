title: "33 Variations"
date: 2018-07-24
---

Theatrical production of Moises Kauffman's "33 Variations", a dramatic interpretation of the composition of Beethoven's [Diabelli Variations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diabelli_Variations), written late in his life, as seen through the eyes of a musicologist suffering from her own ever growing illness.

Produced by [Big Idea Theatre](http://www.bigideatheatre.org/) - July 2015

Scope provided:

- Projection design
- Video production
- Video editing
